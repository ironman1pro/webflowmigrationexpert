Questions

Before you book a call

Do I lose my product data?

No. Product titles, descriptions, images, variants, and collections all carry over as part of the migration, whichever checkout path we land on.

Can I keep using Shopify Payments?

In most setups, yes. If we keep Shopify running for checkout behind a Webflow front end, your existing payment processor and fulfillment stay exactly as they are.

Will my product pages still rank?

That's the point of the redirect map and metadata carryover built into every migration. Nothing changes on Google's end unless a URL actually moves without a redirect, which we won't let happen.

What happens to my Shopify apps?

We audit what each app is actually doing for you. Some get replaced by native Webflow functionality, some stay if you're keeping Shopify for checkout. Nothing gets dropped without a plan for what replaces it.
