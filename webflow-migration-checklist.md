# Webflow Migration SEO Checklist

25 steps to move your website to Webflow without losing Google rankings, from the URL audit to Search Console after launch. Tick them off as you go; your progress is saved.

## Before the migration

- **Crawl the old site** List every live URL, including blog posts, categories, tags, landing pages and PDFs.
- **Export your top pages** Pull your highest-traffic and most-linked pages from Google Search Console and Analytics.
- **Save current SEO data** Record titles, meta descriptions, H1s, alt text and canonical tags for every page.
- **Benchmark rankings and traffic** Note current positions for your key terms so you can compare after launch.
- **Back up the old site** Keep a full export of content and media before anything changes.

## Build in Webflow

- **Plan the URL structure** Keep URLs the same wherever you can. Only change them with a clear reason.
- **Set up CMS collections** Blog posts, categories, authors and case studies with matching fields.
- **Import CMS content** Bulk import via CSV, then spot-check formatting, images and internal links.
- **Recreate forms and integrations** Forms, CRM connections, booking tools and tracking scripts.
- **Build responsive layouts** Check desktop, tablet and mobile before the content review.

## SEO setup

- **Carry over titles and descriptions** Page by page, plus dynamic SEO fields on CMS templates.
- **Add alt text to every image** Including CMS images and background images that carry meaning.
- **Set canonical tags** Set the global canonical URL in Webflow and check key pages.
- **Add structured data** Organization, breadcrumbs, FAQ and article schema where it fits.
- **Check page speed** Compress images, lazy load below the fold and limit custom scripts.

## Launch

- **Add every 301 redirect** Map each old URL that changes to its new path in Webflow's 301 redirects.
- **Test the redirects** Visit a sample of old URLs, top pages first, and confirm one clean 301 hop.
- **Connect the domain and SSL** Point DNS to Webflow and confirm the secure version loads.
- **Check robots.txt and indexing** Make sure the live site isn't blocked from search engines.
- **Submit the new sitemap** Submit sitemap.xml in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

## After launch

- **Monitor Search Console daily** Watch for crawl errors and 404s for the first two weeks.
- **Fix any 404s fast** Add missing redirects as soon as broken URLs show up.
- **Update important backlinks** Ask sites linking to you to point at the new URLs where they changed.
- **Compare rankings and traffic** Check against your benchmark after two and four weeks.
- **Train your team** Show editors how to update pages and CMS items without breaking layouts.

### How do I migrate a website to Webflow without losing SEO?

Crawl and benchmark the old site, keep URLs the same where you can, carry over titles, descriptions and alt text, add a 301 redirect for every URL that changes, submit the new sitemap and monitor Google Search Console for the first few weeks.

### How long does a Webflow migration take?

Most Webflow migrations take 2 to 3 weeks. Larger sites with many CMS items or a full redesign can take longer.

### What is the most common Webflow migration mistake?

Missing 301 redirects. Old URLs that change without a redirect return 404 pages, which loses traffic and the rankings those pages had built up.

### Does this checklist work for WordPress, Wix and Squarespace?

Yes. The steps apply to any platform. The platform-specific details, like WordPress date-based URLs or Wix's missing export, are covered on each migration page of this site.
