Playground Medic: building the whole funnel, not just the landing page

A Figma-to-Webflow build where the brief covered the entire path a visitor takes, not just how the homepage looked.

IndustryHealthcare
Built fromFigma design
ServicesFigma Design, Webflow Development, Funnel Optimization

The challenge

A polished homepage doesn't mean much if the path after it loses people. Playground Medic's brief wasn't just a design to build, it was a funnel to get right: how a visitor moves from landing on the site to actually booking or converting, and where most builds quietly drop that ball between screens.

The approach

Every screen in the Figma file was mapped to where it sat in the funnel before any Webflow build work started, so the build reflected the intended path, not just a set of disconnected pages that happened to look good individually.

  • Every call-to-action checked against where it actually needed to lead, not just styled to match the design
  • Form and booking steps built and tested end to end, not just the entry point
  • Mobile flow tested separately from desktop, since that's usually where a funnel actually breaks

The result

A live site where the design and the funnel behind it were built as one thing, not a homepage that looks good followed by an afterthought path to conversion. The client got a site that matches their Figma design exactly and a path to booking that was designed on purpose.
