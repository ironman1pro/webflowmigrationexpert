Questions

Before you book a call

How long does a migration actually take?

Most single-platform migrations take 2–6 weeks depending on how many pages, products, or blog posts need to move, and how custom the new design is. You'll get a firm timeline after the audit, not a guess.

Will I lose my Google rankings?

Not if the migration is done properly. That means a full URL redirect map, matching metadata and headings, and a technical check after launch, not just copying content over and hoping. This is the part most DIY migrations get wrong.

Do you migrate my existing content, or do I have to redo it?

Your pages, blog posts, products, and images move over as part of the migration. You won't need to retype or re-upload anything that already exists on your current site.

What does a migration cost?

It depends on the size of your site and how much of the design changes versus staying the same. You'll get a clear scope and price after the free call, no surprise line items after the fact.

Can you match my current design, or will it look completely different?

Either. Some clients want a pixel-accurate rebuild of what they already have, just faster and easier to edit. Others use the migration as a chance to redesign. Your call, we'll talk through it before anything is built.

Do I need a Webflow account already?

No. If you don't have one, setting it up is part of the process, and you'll own the account and the site fully once we hand it off.
