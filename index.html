Questions

Before you book a call

How long does a migration actually take?

Most single-platform migrations take 2–6 weeks, depending on page count and how custom the new design is. You'll get a firm timeline after the audit, not a guess.

Will I lose my Google rankings?

Not if it's done properly: a full redirect map, matching metadata, and a technical check after launch. This is the part most DIY migrations get wrong.

Do you migrate my existing content, or do I have to redo it?

Your pages, blog posts, products, and images move over as part of the migration. Nothing needs to be retyped or re-uploaded.

What does a migration cost?

Depends on your site's size and how much the design changes. You'll get a clear scope and price after the free call, no surprises after.

Can you match my current design, or will it look completely different?

Either. Some clients want a pixel-accurate rebuild, just faster and easier to edit. Others redesign while they're at it. Your call.

Do I need a Webflow account already?

No. Setting one up is part of the process, and you own it fully once the site is handed off.
