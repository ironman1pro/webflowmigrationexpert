Framer to Webflow in short

How do you migrate from Framer to Webflow?

Quick answer

A Framer to Webflow migration rebuilds the design in Webflow, recreates animations with Webflow Interactions or GSAP, moves Framer CMS collections into Webflow CMS with matching fields, carries over page SEO settings, and adds 301 redirects for any URL that changes. Most Framer to Webflow migrations take 2 to 3 weeks.

The benefits

Why move from Framer to Webflow

01

A more powerful CMS

Webflow CMS supports reference fields, multi-reference fields and filtering for complex content like case studies and resource libraries.

02

Built for marketing teams

Webflow's editing tools let non-designers update content safely, with clear roles and permissions.

03

Deeper SEO control

Per-page and per-collection SEO settings, schema through custom code, and full redirect management.

04

A larger ecosystem

Webflow has a large marketplace of apps, integrations and experienced developers.

Where migrations go wrong

Framer to Webflow pitfalls I plan around

Pitfall 01

Animation parity

Framer makes rich motion easy, and a careless migration loses it. I rebuild interactions deliberately so the site still feels premium.

Pitfall 02

Components and variants

Framer components don't convert automatically. I rebuild them as Webflow components so your team can reuse them.

Pitfall 03

CMS structure

Framer CMS fields don't always map one to one. I design the Webflow CMS structure first, then import, so your content is easy to manage later.

FAQ

Framer to Webflow questions, answered

More general questions are answered on the Webflow migration FAQ.

Can I export my Framer site to Webflow?

+

There's no one-click export from Framer to Webflow. The design is rebuilt in Webflow and CMS content is moved into Webflow CMS, which gives you a cleaner, more maintainable site.

Will my Framer animations work in Webflow?

+

Yes. Animations are recreated with Webflow Interactions or GSAP to match the original feel as closely as possible.

Will I lose SEO moving from Framer to Webflow?

+

No, as long as URLs and SEO settings are handled carefully. Every changed URL gets a 301 redirect and all page SEO settings are carried over.

How long does a Framer to Webflow migration take?

+

Most Framer to Webflow migrations take 2 to 3 weeks, depending on page count, CMS size and animation complexity.

Why move from Framer to Webflow?

+

Teams usually move for a more powerful CMS, better editing for marketers, deeper SEO control and a larger ecosystem of integrations and developers.
