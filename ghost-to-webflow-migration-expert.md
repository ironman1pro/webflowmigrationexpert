## What does a Ghost to Webflow migration expert do?

**Quick answer**

A Ghost to Webflow migration expert plans and runs the whole move for you so nothing breaks. A Ghost to Webflow migration exports your Ghost content as JSON, imports posts, tags and authors into Webflow CMS collections, rebuilds the theme in Webflow, carries over SEO settings and adds 301 redirects. Ghost posts usually live at the root (/post-slug/) while Webflow CMS items live under a collection path (/blog/post-slug), so every post needs a mapped redirect. Most Ghost to Webflow migrations take 2 to 3 weeks.
