What does a Duda to Webflow migration expert do?

Quick answer

A Duda to Webflow migration expert plans and runs the whole move for you so nothing breaks. A Duda to Webflow migration rebuilds the design in Webflow, moves page content and blog posts into Webflow and Webflow CMS, re-hosts images, recreates widgets with Webflow features or integrations, carries over SEO settings and adds 301 redirects for changed URLs. Most Duda to Webflow migrations take 2 to 3 weeks.
