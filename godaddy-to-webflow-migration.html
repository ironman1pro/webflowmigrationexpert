GoDaddy to Webflow in short

How do you move a GoDaddy website to Webflow?

Quick answer

A GoDaddy to Webflow migration rebuilds the site in Webflow, collects page content and any blog posts from GoDaddy Website Builder, re-hosts images, carries over SEO settings and adds 301 redirects for changed URLs. Your domain can stay registered at GoDaddy and simply point to Webflow. Most GoDaddy to Webflow migrations take 2 to 3 weeks.

Where migrations go wrong

GoDaddy to Webflow pitfalls I plan around

Pitfall 01

No export

GoDaddy Website Builder doesn't export sites. I collect every page and asset before rebuilding.

Pitfall 02

DNS switch

Pointing a GoDaddy domain to Webflow needs the right records. I handle the DNS change to avoid downtime and email issues.

Pitfall 03

Email records

If your email runs through your domain, its records must stay intact. I check them before touching DNS.

FAQ

GoDaddy to Webflow questions, answered

More general questions are answered on the Webflow migration FAQ.

Can I keep my domain at GoDaddy and use Webflow?

+

Yes. Your domain can stay registered at GoDaddy. Its DNS records are updated to point to Webflow.

Will my email stop working if I move to Webflow?

+

No. Email records are kept as they are. Only the records for your website change.

Will I lose SEO moving from GoDaddy to Webflow?

+

No, with proper 301 redirects and SEO settings carried over.

How long does a GoDaddy to Webflow migration take?

+

Most GoDaddy to Webflow migrations take 2 to 3 weeks.

Can you redesign the site at the same time?

+

Yes. The migration is a good moment for a redesign, and both can happen together.
