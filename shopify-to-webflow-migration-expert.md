A Shopify to Webflow migration expert plans and runs the whole move for you so nothing breaks. It depends on your store. Smaller catalogs can move fully to Webflow Ecommerce for more design freedom. Larger stores often keep Shopify for checkout and move the marketing site, blog and landing pages to Webflow. ### Why hire a Shopify to Webflow migration expert instead of doing it yourself?

Most traffic lost in a migration comes from broken URLs and missing SEO data, not from the design. A Shopify to Webflow migration expert maps every URL, sets up 301 redirects, carries over titles, descriptions and alt text, and watches Google Search Console after launch, so your rankings survive the move.

### Can Webflow replace Shopify?

For smaller catalogs, Webflow Ecommerce can replace Shopify. For larger stores, many brands keep Shopify for checkout and use Webflow for the brand and content site.

### Will I lose SEO moving from Shopify to Webflow?

No, with a proper redirect plan. Every product, collection and page URL that changes gets a 301 redirect, and SEO settings are carried over.

### How long does a Shopify to Webflow migration take?

A marketing site usually takes 2 to 3 weeks. Full store migrations depend on catalog size, and you get an exact timeline with your quote.

### Can I keep Shopify checkout with a Webflow site?

Yes. A hybrid setup keeps Shopify for products and checkout while Webflow runs your brand site, blog and landing pages.

### Can you move my Shopify blog to Webflow?

Yes. Blog posts move into Webflow CMS with images, authors and SEO fields.
