GoDaddy Website Builder doesn't export sites. I collect every page and asset before rebuilding.

### DNS switch

Pointing a GoDaddy domain to Webflow needs the right records. I handle the DNS change to avoid downtime and email issues.

### Email records

If your email runs through your domain, its records must stay intact. I check them before touching DNS.
