WordPress to Webflow in short

How do you migrate a WordPress site to Webflow?

Quick answer

A WordPress to Webflow migration has five parts: export your posts and pages (WordPress XML or CSV), rebuild the design in Webflow, import posts into Webflow CMS collections with categories and tags as reference fields, recreate SEO titles, descriptions and alt text from Yoast or Rank Math, and add 301 redirects for every URL that changes. Most WordPress to Webflow migrations take 2 to 3 weeks.

The benefits

Why move from WordPress to Webflow

01

No more plugin maintenance

Webflow has hosting, security, SSL, backups and forms built in, so there are no plugins to update or conflicts to debug.

02

Faster pages

Page builders like Elementor and Divi add heavy markup. A clean Webflow build loads faster and scores better on Core Web Vitals.

03

Editing without a developer

Your marketing team edits text, images and blog posts visually, without breaking the layout.

04

Fewer security worries

No PHP, no database and no third-party plugins exposed to attacks.

Where migrations go wrong

WordPress to Webflow pitfalls I plan around

Pitfall 01

Date-based and nested URLs

WordPress URLs like /2024/05/post-name/ can't be reproduced in Webflow, where CMS items live at /collection/item. I map every old URL and use Webflow's pattern-based 301 redirects so none of them break.

Pitfall 02

Shortcodes and page builder markup

Elementor, Divi and shortcode content doesn't export cleanly. I clean it up so your posts arrive as proper rich text, not broken code.

Pitfall 03

Plugin features

Popups, sliders, search and membership plugins each need a Webflow equivalent or an integration. I list them all during the audit, so nothing disappears at launch.

FAQ

WordPress to Webflow questions, answered

More general questions are answered on the Webflow migration FAQ.

Will I lose my Google rankings moving from WordPress to Webflow?

+

Not if the migration is planned properly. Every URL is mapped and 301 redirected, and your Yoast or Rank Math titles, descriptions and alt text are carried over. I also monitor Google Search Console after launch to catch crawl errors early.

Can you migrate my WordPress blog to Webflow CMS?

+

Yes. Posts are exported from WordPress and bulk imported into Webflow CMS with their slugs, images, authors, categories and SEO fields.

How long does a WordPress to Webflow migration take?

+

Most WordPress to Webflow migrations take 2 to 3 weeks. Very large blogs or sites with complex plugins can take longer, and you get an exact timeline with your quote.

What happens to my WordPress plugins?

+

Webflow covers most plugin jobs natively: SEO settings, forms, hosting, security and backups. Anything else gets a Webflow feature, an integration or a small custom script.

Can you move WooCommerce to Webflow?

+

Smaller stores can move to Webflow Ecommerce. For larger catalogs I'll recommend the best setup during the audit, before any work starts.
