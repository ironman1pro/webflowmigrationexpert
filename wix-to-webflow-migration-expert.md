### Wix URL patterns

Wix blog posts live at /post/slug and other pages use Wix-specific paths. Each one gets a 301 redirect to its new Webflow URL.

**Problem 03**

### Images hosted on Wix

Images served from Wix's servers disappear when you cancel your plan. I download and re-host every image in Webflow before launch.

## What does a Wix to Webflow migration expert do?

**Quick answer**

A Wix to Webflow migration expert plans and runs the whole move for you so nothing breaks. Wix has no export feature, so a Wix to Webflow migration is a rebuild: the design is recreated in Webflow, blog posts and content are extracted and imported into Webflow CMS, images are re-hosted, and every Wix URL (such as /post/your-article) gets a 301 redirect to its new Webflow URL.
